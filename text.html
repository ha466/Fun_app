Lord of Mysteries Anime Guide

The Fool & The Fool Pathway

World Overview

Setting & Geography

Lord of Mysteries is set in a dark steampunk fantasy world during the Fifth Epoch, also known as the Iron Age (Year 1349). The world resembles a Victorian-era Europe layered with hidden mystical societies, cosmic horror elements, and supernatural hierarchies. The primary setting focuses on the Northern Continent, though the world consists of four continents populated by various nations and cultures.

Major Nations on the Northern Continent:

  • Loen Kingdom: A major industrial power serving as a primary setting for the story, featuring the capital city of Backland, which resembles Victorian-era London. This kingdom blends steampunk technology with hidden supernatural elements.
  • Inis Republic: A nation that mirrors Paris, with strong remnants of Emperor Roselle's historical influence. The republic maintains complex political structures and occult connections.
  • Ruen: Another significant nation with its own governmental structures and beyonder organizations.

Key Cities:

  • Tening City: A bustling metropolis with a population of several hundred thousand, located within the Loen Kingdom. This is the primary setting where the protagonist awakens.
  • Backland: The capital of the Loen Kingdom, a major center of power, politics, and mysticism.
  • City of Silver: An isolated forsaken land cut off from the rest of the world, containing ancient knowledge and rare materials from the Second Epoch.

The Seven Orthodox Gods

At the start of the Fifth Epoch, seven main gods dominate the Northern Continent and hold immense religious and political power:

  1. Evernight Goddess - Authority over night, dreams, and darkness
  2. Eternal Blazing Sun - Authority over light, purification, and justice
  3. Lord of Storms - Authority over weather and natural disasters
  4. God of Knowledge and Wisdom - Authority over intellect and secrets
  5. Earth Mother - Authority over nature and fertility
  6. God of Combat - Authority over warfare and strength
  7. God of Steam and Machinery - Authority over technology and industry

Each god possesses a powerful church with loyal followers and shape political, religious, and mystical power struggles across the Northern Continent. These churches maintain strict hierarchies and control vast networks of resources and information.

Epochs & History

The world operates on a system of Epochs, representing major historical periods. The Fifth Epoch spans from approximately year 1 to year 1400+. Each epoch is shaped by the actions of ancient beings and historical events. The story begins with Zhou Mingrui's transmigration into Klein Moretti's body, triggering events that will reshape the world's destiny.

The Fool (Klein Moretti)

Background & Transmigration

Klein Moretti is the vessel through which Zhou Mingrui, an ordinary Chinese youth from modern Earth, transmigrates into a Victorian-era fantasy world. Zhou performs an unknown ritual that triggers his transmigration into Klein's body after Klein's mysterious suicide. This event serves as the catalyst for the entire narrative.

Initially, Zhou (as Klein) struggles to conceal his transmigration while discovering that Klein's suicide was not truly a suicide but influenced by unknown supernatural forces. Klein inherits fragments of Klein's original memories, including knowledge of languages, family relationships, and Klein's connections to the beyonder world.

Initial Powers & Development

Sequence 9 - Seer (Starting Point)

Upon discovering Klein's body as a Beyonder, Zhou gains access to the foundational abilities of the Fool Pathway:

  • Spirit Vision: The ability to perceive spiritual bodies, auras, and invisible entities in the physical and spiritual world
  • Enhanced Perception & Intuition: Heightened awareness of supernatural phenomena and mystical disturbances
  • Minor Divination Powers: Rudimentary divination capabilities to glimpse potential futures or hidden truths
  • Danger Intuition: A built-in spiritual sense that allows Zhou to intuitively detect danger using his spirituality
  • Acting Method Foundation: The philosophical foundation that a Seer must show humility before fate, observing and aligning with destiny rather than challenging it directly

Journey to Godhood

Klein's progression through the Fool Pathway involves consuming potions at each sequence level and performing dangerous rituals to advance. Each advancement grants exponentially more powerful abilities while increasing the risk of losing control or descending into madness.

Key Character Traits Throughout Advancement:

  • Cautiousness & Intelligence: Klein maintains careful planning and strategic thinking despite mounting power
  • Gentlemanly Demeanor: Even as a god, Klein remains polite and respectful in his interactions
  • Good-Natured Humor: He uses humor and wit to navigate dangerous supernatural conflicts
  • Lover of Justice: Fundamentally motivated by a desire for fairness and protection of innocents
  • Acting Excellence: His mastery of performing and adopting identities allows him to manipulate situations and fool both mortals and gods

The Gray Fog (Sefirah Castle)

One of Klein's most significant discoveries is access to the Gray Fog, a mysterious interdimensional space above the mortal realm. This space becomes foundational to his power and serves multiple purposes:

Functions of the Gray Fog:

  • Safe Haven: A location where Klein can retreat from external threats and supernatural interference
  • Divination Enhancement: Within the Gray Fog, Klein can amplify his divination abilities significantly, overcoming most magical obstacles
  • Madness Protection: The Gray Fog shields Klein's mind from the corrosive effects of supernatural knowledge and possession attempts
  • Storage & Transfer: A pocket dimension where Klein can store items and transfer objects across locations
  • Tarot Club Meeting Chamber: The primary meeting space for Klein's organization
  • Acting Method Support: Enhanced connection to historical knowledge and past identities

The Gray Fog represents Klein's access to Sefirah Castle, a mysterious divine structure above the normal world connected to the Fool Pathway's ultimate authority.
